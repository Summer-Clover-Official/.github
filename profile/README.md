## 🌞 About This Game  

**💗 Summer Clover 💗**  
**An Unexpected Transfer...**  

You are millions of dollars in debt and struggling to make ends meet as you toil away at your day job for a major conglomerate. Your life takes a frustrating turn for the worse when your colleagues repeatedly and falsely accuse you of sexual harassment.  

In a twist of fate, your supervisor **Shizuko** proposes an unexpected job transfer—a position as a **live-in housekeeper** at your employer's mansion! Along with a **significant pay raise**, you’ll also be given **free room and board!**  

However, you'll be spending your time serving the owners of the mansion, who happen to be **three unusually captivating sisters...**  

Prepare for a summer full of **blush-worthy, heart-pounding escapades** as you tend to the three sisters and your dependable supervisor.
